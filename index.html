SkillConnect Official is an independent Indian skill-based services marketplace connecting customers, verified service providers, freelancers, and companies across India. For Customers & Businesses Seeking Trusted Talent

Customers, startups, and enterprises across India use SkillConnect Official to discover verified professionals backed by real project portfolios rather than unverified claims. Whether you need software engineering, UI/UX design, technical consulting, digital marketing, or 1-on-1 mentorship, SkillConnect connects you directly with skilled Indian practitioners.

  • Browse verified service provider profiles with real project links and work proof
  • Submit direct service inquiries and project collaboration requests with zero middleman fees
  • Book structured 1-on-1 mentorship, code reviews, and technical consultation sessions

For Skilled Service Providers, Freelancers & Creators

Service providers, independent professionals, and students across India can register on SkillConnect Official to showcase verified capabilities, build cryptographic skill portfolios, and connect directly with customers and hiring companies.

  • Create verifiable portfolios showcasing live demonstrations, repositories, and case studies
  • Participate in peer-to-peer skill exchange and barter skills with fellow Indian creators
  • Receive direct customer inquiries, freelance gigs, and micro-apprenticeship opportunities

Core Services & Platform Capabilities
